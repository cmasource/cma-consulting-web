## Style Prompt

Premium vertical Instagram Story launch piece for CMA Consulting. The visual system should feel like a modern business operating system: deep navy canvas, teal diagnostic signals, elegant white typography, fine grid lines, clean panels, soft glow, and precise motion. The protagonist is the system transformation from operational disorder to management clarity.

## Colors

- `#06111F` - deep navy canvas
- `#0A1E32` - elevated panel navy
- `#102D45` - secondary panel surface
- `#12D6C4` - teal signal and connections
- `#7DEFE4` - active highlight
- `#EEF8F8` - primary text
- `#A8BDC6` - secondary text
- `#2B5268` - grid and fine borders

## Typography

- Main text: system `sans-serif`
- Technical labels only: system `monospace`

## What NOT to Do

- No people, avatars, stock assets, logos from other brands, or generic startup visuals.
- No overloaded dashboard UI, tiny text, or dense data.
- No fast camera moves, harsh flashes, chaotic motion, or excessive glow.
- No `cma_source` as protagonist; the brand is `CMA Consulting`.
