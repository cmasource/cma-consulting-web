## Style Prompt

Premium B2B consulting motion graphic for CMA Consulting: sober, dark, precise, and modern. The frame should feel like a high-end operational diagnostic system, with generous safe margins, large readable Spanish text, subtle grid structure, teal signal motion, and quiet glow. The composition is a native 1920x1080 video canvas, not a webpage capture.

## Colors

- `#06111F` - deep navy canvas
- `#0B2035` - panel navy
- `#12D6C4` - diagnostic teal signal
- `#7DEFE4` - bright teal highlight
- `#DDEBEE` - primary text
- `#8FA8B4` - secondary text
- `#284B63` - restrained borders and grid

## Typography

- Display and body: system `sans-serif`
- Data labels: system `monospace`

## What NOT to Do

- No stock assets, people, logos, or decorative images.
- No tiny dashboards or dense UI details.
- No fast camera moves, chaotic particle movement, or aggressive flashes.
- No generic startup copy or random filler text.
